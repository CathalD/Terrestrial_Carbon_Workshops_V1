# Terrestrial Carbon Workshops

Here you will find workshop materials for measuring ecosystem carbon in Canadian terrestrial ecosystems: Including Forest, wetlands, grasslands, and Arctic circumpolar regions

You can find the links to each workshop and what they entail below:
| | Workshop | Pools measured ||
|---|---|---|---|
| 🌲 | [**Forests**](Forests/) | Trees · soil · understory *(+ optional LiDAR)* | |
| 🌾 | [**Grasslands**](Grasslands/) | Soil · **roots** · shrubs · herbaceous vegetation *(+ optional monitoring)* | |
| 💧 | [**Wetlands**](Wetlands/) | Peat · vegetation *(+ optional peat chronology)* | |

---

## Each Workshop is organized into four general sections:

| # | Section | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Background** | What the ecosystem's carbon is, which pools hold it, and why it matters |
| 2 | **Project Planning** | *Setting up a successful Carbon measurement project* — how many plots, where, and what are you measuring |
| 3 | **Field Methods** | *Collecting the data* What tools and techniques you will need |
| 4 | **Data Interpretation** | Lab work, the carbon calculator, scaling and reporting |

In addition to these four essential components, there are extra workshops attached to each section that one can add to a project:

| Ecosystem Type |  | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| 🌲 Forests | **[LiDAR Supplement](Forests/05_LiDAR_Supplement/)** | Carries a plot-based estimate across a whole block as a **map**, where LiDAR coverage exists |
| 🌾 Grasslands | **[Monitoring Supplement](Grasslands/05_Monitoring/)** | Turns a stock into a **baseline** — what it takes to detect **change** on a second visit, and what to measure instead when the stock will take decades to move |
| 💧 Wetlands | **[Chronology Supplement](Wetlands/05_Chronology_Supplement/)** | Turns a stock into a **rate** — peat is its own dated archive, so one visit can answer how fast carbon accumulated |
